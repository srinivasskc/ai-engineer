[FREQUENTLY_ASKED_QUESTIONS]

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login screen, click "Forgot Password", and enter your registered email address. You will receive a reset link within 5 minutes.

Q: Can I recover a deleted file?
A: Yes. Log into your web dashboard, navigate to the "Trash" folder, select the file, and click "Restore". Files remain in the trash for 30 days before permanent deletion.

Q: What should I do if sync is paused?
A: Check your internet connection first. Click the CloudSync Pro tray icon, go to Settings, and click "Resume Sync". If the issue continues, restart the application.

[TROUBLESHOOTING_STEPS]
Error Code E-101 (Connection Timeout):
1. Verify internet or local network connectivity.
2. Ensure corporate firewalls or VPNs do not block port 443.
3. Restart the CloudSync Pro background service.
